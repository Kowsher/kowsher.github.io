Md Kowsher

Research Scientist, MetaPhD Student, University of Central Florida

I am a Research Scientist at Meta, where I work on multimodal and agentic LLMs for Facebook Trust & Safety, focusing on contextual understanding and reasoning. I am also a PhD student in Computer Science at the Center for Research in Computer Vision (CRCV), UCF, advised by Prof. Chen Chen.

My research focuses on efficient adaptation of large models — parameter-efficient fine-tuning, mixture-of-experts, and memory-efficient training — and on multimodal learning across text, image, and video.

Previously, I was an ML Research Intern at Apple (2026), a Multimodal LLM Intern at Meta (2025), a Research Scientist Intern at Nokia Bell Labs (2024), an Amazon Alexa Prize Researcher (2022–2023, 2nd place in SocialBot Grand Challenge 5), and an Applied Scientist Intern at Amazon (2022). I received my MSc in Computer Science from Stevens Institute of Technology, advised by Prof. Jia Xu.
